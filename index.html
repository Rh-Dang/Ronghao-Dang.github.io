04 Selected Projects

Research highlights

Selected projects.

01 / FOUNDATION MODELFirst Contributor · Team Leader

RynnBrain

One unified framework for comprehensive egocentric understanding, diverse spatiotemporal localization, physically grounded reasoning, and physics-aware planning.

  • 2B, 8B, and 30B-A3B MoE model family evaluated across 28 benchmarks.
  • Outperforms models including Gemini-ER 1.5 and Cosmos-Reason2 in the reported evaluation.
  • Four post-trained variants: RynnBrain-Nav, RynnBrain-Plan, RynnBrain-VLA, and RynnBrain-CoP.
02 / EMBODIED COGNITIONFirst Contributor · Team Leader

RynnEC

A region-centric video paradigm for embodied-agent cognition, enabling fine-grained perception and more precise physical interaction.

  • Targets object cognition, object segmentation, and spatial reasoning.
  • Reported a 23.5% improvement over Gemini 2.5 Pro on the evaluated embodied cognition tasks.
03 / NAVIGATIONFirst Contributor

Visual Object Navigation

Introduced DOA, DAT, and MT to strengthen indoor object navigation.

  • Improved the reported indoor object-navigation success rate from 60% to 80%.
